# Goal wording (register 511)

A goal is an order to the player, in plain verbs that take an object, never a fact that reads as already happening. Draft, for his review; nothing in the game changed yet.

The tutorial card: "Robot reaches the star." becomes "Get the robot to the star."

The goal card's short rows (with icons) would follow the same verbs, and the three every level has: "at least 4 moves" becomes "take 4 or more moves", "exactly 6" becomes "take exactly 6 moves", "3×2 or smaller" becomes "fit it in 3×2 or smaller".
