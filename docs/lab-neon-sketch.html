wee in a lab: neon on grey, one pixel size (registers 596, 598, 599)

Redrawn after "still inconsistent: apply gestalt and guarantee pixel size consistency". Everything on both screens below, room, HUD, icons and letters, is drawn on one grid: 1 art pixel = 2 screen pixels (doubled in register 599: tiles and icons are 16x16, redrawn with more detail rather than scaled up), through one drawing function that throws on any position or size that is not a whole art pixel. Text uses a 5x7 pixel font drawn on the same grid, so letters are made of the same pixels as the blob.

The rules this sketch follows

RuleHow it shows
One pixel sizeAll art at 2 px per pixel. Tiles and icons are both 16x16; letters are 5x7 with a 1-pixel gap; icons share one 2-pixel stroke. No scaled sprites, no smooth fonts, no half pixels.
Common region (gestalt)Things that belong together share one framed panel: the top bar, the goal card, the tool bar. Nothing floats outside a panel except the room.
ProximityTwo gaps only: 2 pixels between items of one group (undo, redo, reset), 6 between groups. The eye reads the groups without labels.
SimilaritySame job, same shape: every button is a 20x20 panel with a 16x16 icon; every chip is 13 pixels tall; every room on the map is the same size for its kind.
AlignmentA 4-pixel outer margin and one left edge for every panel; the room is centred on the same axis as the panels.
Figure and groundGreys are ground; neon is figure, and only what acts is neon. One neon button on screen at a time: the thing to do next.
One frame weightEvery frame is 1 pixel; every corner is the same 2-step stair. A thicker frame never stands for importance: colour does.

The palette

The play screen

  • Top bar (one panel): wordmark and level number left; levels, Run, Test right as three 20x20 buttons. Run is the only neon button.
  • Goal card (one panel, purple frame): the big star, the main goals as chips beside it; under a 1-pixel rule, the side goals as chips, each led by its lamp (dark, lit, or lit and ringed: kept, met by this room).
  • Room: 7x5 tiles of 16 pixels, centred.
  • Tool bar (one panel): the chosen tool's name; then two groups side by side, history (undo, redo, reset) and tools (look, erase, move); under them the pieces. Chosen piece: cyan frame.

The campaign floor plan (rules of 558; chambers 1 and 2 beaten, 3 next, ★ 5)

  • Three sizes only: build chambers 36x28, side rooms 28x20, facilities 48x28. Side rooms carry the HUD's own icons: the arrow pad (steer, puzzle) and a crate (supply, challenge). Same kind, same size, everywhere.
  • Build chambers alternate sides of one corridor; each one's side room (cyan steer, orange supply) sits on its outer wall, touching it, so the pair reads as one place (proximity).
  • Side-goal lamps are 5x5 pixels in a row over the chamber, dark grey until beaten.
  • Locked rooms: frame and number in the darkest grey; open: light grey frame; next: purple.
