wee in a lab: neon on grey, one pixel size (registers 596, 598)

Redrawn after "still inconsistent: apply gestalt and guarantee pixel size consistency". Everything on both screens below, room, HUD, icons and letters, is drawn on one grid: 1 art pixel = 4 screen pixels, through one drawing function that throws on any position or size that is not a whole art pixel. Text uses a 3x5 pixel font drawn on the same grid, so letters are made of the same pixels as the blob.

The rules this sketch follows

RuleHow it shows
One pixel sizeAll art at 4 px per pixel. Tiles and icons are both 8x8; letters are 3x5 with a 1-pixel gap. No scaled sprites, no smooth fonts, no half pixels.
Common region (gestalt)Things that belong together share one framed panel: the top bar, the goal card, the tool bar. Nothing floats outside a panel except the room.
ProximityTwo gaps only: 1 pixel between items of one group (undo, redo, reset touch almost), 3 pixels between groups. The eye reads the groups without labels.
SimilaritySame job, same shape: every button is a 10x10 notched square with an 8x8 icon; every chip is 7 pixels tall; every room on the map is the same size for its kind.
AlignmentA 2-pixel outer margin and one left edge for every panel; the room is centred on the same axis as the panels.
Figure and groundGreys are ground; neon is figure, and only what acts is neon. One neon button on screen at a time: the thing to do next.
One frame weightEvery frame is 1 pixel; every corner notch is 1 pixel. A thicker frame never stands for importance: colour does.

The palette

The play screen

  • Top bar (one panel): wordmark and level number left; levels, Run, Test right as three 10x10 buttons. Run is the only neon button.
  • Goal card (one panel, purple frame): the big star, the main goals as chips beside it; under a 1-pixel rule, the side goals as chips, each led by its lamp (dark, lit, or lit and ringed: kept, met by this room).
  • Room: 7x5 tiles of 8 pixels, centred.
  • Tool bar (one panel): the chosen tool's name; then two groups side by side, history (undo, redo, reset) and tools (look, erase, move); under them the pieces. Chosen piece: cyan frame.

The campaign floor plan (rules of 558; chambers 1 and 2 beaten, 3 next, ★ 5)

  • Three sizes only: build chambers 18x14, side rooms 14x10, facilities 24x14. Side rooms carry the HUD's own icons: the arrow pad (steer, puzzle) and a crate (supply, challenge). Same kind, same size, everywhere.
  • Build chambers alternate sides of one corridor; each one's side room (cyan steer, orange supply) sits on its outer wall, touching it, so the pair reads as one place (proximity).
  • Side-goal lamps are 2x2 pixels in a row over the chamber, dark grey until beaten.
  • Locked rooms: frame and number in the darkest grey; open: light grey frame; next: purple.
