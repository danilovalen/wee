wee in a lab: neon on grey (register 596)

After "a bit more neon on gray, think monokai; HUD should reflect it and pixel too". Monokai's rule, carried over: everything that only sits there is a neutral warm grey, and neon goes only on what acts or matters (the blob, the fruit, doors and switches, the beam, the stars, the one thing you can tap next). The HUD uses the same greys, hard pixel edges, notched corners and a pixel font. Words are drafts; "stars" stays the score (register 597).

The palette

D. Neon lab

Greys from Monokai Pro's warm neutrals; neons from its six accents, each given one job so no two things on screen share a colour.

The play screen with a pixel HUD

  • Top bar: the pixel wordmark, the level number, then the three actions as notched pixel buttons: levels, Run (the one neon button: it is what you do next), Test.
  • Goal card: a dark panel with a 2-pixel purple frame. The big star sits left with the main goals; side goals are chips, each with a lamp that is dark, lit, or ringed (kept, met now; register 586).
  • The room: 8x8 tiles drawn 4 px per pixel; floor lines one grey step from the floor, so the grid reads without competing.
  • Bottom bar: undo, redo, reset, then look, erase, move, then the pieces you may place; the chosen tool gets a cyan frame.
  • Type: one pixel font (Silkscreen here) at whole-pixel sizes only, so letters stay sharp.
  • Icons: 8x8 pixel drawings in the HUD grey, the same grid as the tiles; no emoji, no outlines at fractional sizes.

The campaign floor plan in neon (rules of 558, the same moment: chambers 1 and 2 beaten, 3 next, ★ 5)

  • Walls and rooms are greys; a room you can enter now has a neon frame, locked ones none.
  • Steer rooms (puzzles) are cyan, supply rooms (challenges) orange, build chambers purple: the three gameplays by colour, everywhere the same.
  • Side-goal lamps over each beaten chamber; dark ones stay grey.
  • Stockroom, annex, workbench and lab notes as before, in grey until something there is new, which lights its sign.
