wee in a lab: palettes and the campaign map (registers 593-595)

The robot becomes a gelatinous blob, a petri-dish culture that a lab sends through tests; the star becomes a piece of fruit. The lab is clean and a little clinical, like Portal or Mind over Magnet, and the blob is the soft, squeezy thing in it. Every room below is drawn in 8x8 pixel tiles with 3 shades per material, the way a pixel-art version would be built. Names and words are drafts.

Three palettes, the same room

The room: the blob top left, a fruit to reach, blocks, a box, a door with its switch, a laser and its beam. Each palette keeps the rules that make the game readable: the floor is quiet, solid things are darker than the floor, the blob and the fruit are the two loudest colours and never share a hue, and the beam is the only saturated warning colour.

The campaign map: the lab's floor plan

The rules of 558 do not change (beating a test chamber opens its side goals, the next chamber, and one puzzle or challenge; the first use of a piece adds it to the editor; stars open rewards and packs only). This is the same moment of play as in progression-sketch.html: chambers 1 and 2 beaten, chamber 3 next, ★ 5. Drawn in palette A.

How the three gameplays sit on the floor plan

  • Build chambers (today's levels) are the rooms along the main corridor, numbered, opened one by one. You build the room; the blob runs it.
  • Beating a build chamber unlocks a door in its side wall into a small room: a steer room (puzzle: you move the blob yourself) or a supply room (challenge: a half-built dish and a crate of given pieces). Before the win that wall is plain.
  • A chamber's side goals are indicator lamps over its door, dark until the chamber is beaten, then lit one by one as they are met.
  • The stockroom holds the rewards: blob skins (gel colours), block finishes, palettes, unusual tools. Each shelf shows its star number.
  • Annex wings are the packs, behind keycard doors that read a star total. The corridor itself never needs stars.
  • The workbench is the editor (Sandbox). A piece the blob meets for the first time arrives on its bench: "Box added to the workbench".
  • Lab notes on the wall are the achievements, one note per finding.
  • The blob moves around the map the way it moves in a chamber, sliding until something stops it, so the map is played, not tapped.

The same rules as an incubator rack (the track, themed)

  • Each build chamber is a petri dish on a shelf, one per row, read bottom to top; the dish to play next glows.
  • A beaten dish gets a small dish beside it: its steer or supply test.
  • Side goals are dots on the dish's label, empty until the dish is beaten.
  • The rewards line is the incubator's display at the top.
Map or rack: the floor plan carries more of the theme and the "place" feeling of Baba Is You; the rack is the Duolingo track with a lab coat on, the easiest to read on a phone.
