4. A calmer header and one palette

Shown in every proposed frame above. The header keeps the logo, Levels, the level number, and play: ▶ for Run (■ to stop) and ✋ for Test (play it yourself). Undo, redo and Reset move down beside the tools, and tools and pieces share one row, with small gaps between the three groups instead of a "LOOK" label.

Open questions

  1. Run and Test as two icons (▶ and ✋), or one play button? The benchmark leans to one, but Test (playing it yourself) is a real second action in wee.
  2. Where the full sentence of a goal lives once the card is gone: on the level list card, or as a long-press on its icon? (A long-press shows a label over the icon; nothing on the screen moves.)
  3. The resize arrows: keep the eight around the grid, or show them only while a Resize tool is picked? The second changes the screen with the tool, which may break the "nothing opens or closes" rule.
  4. "Reset" as an icon (⟳) is easy to mistake for redo. Keep it as a word, or move it to the level list?
