Two win moments per level

Sketch for register row 415, drawn over a real level 7 screen. Level 7 has four side goals: exactly 6 moves, a 4×4 room or smaller, the box on the button at the end, and any two at once.

When each card shows

Rules

CaseWhat shows
First win, some starsCard 1.
First win that also meets every side goalCard 2 only. Never two cards in a row.
A later run with a new starThe one line, as above. A star counts once met in any run; they need not all come in one run.
The run that brings the last starCard 2.
Coming back to a finished levelNo cards. Runs show the verdict, as now.
Next level on the last levelBecomes "Levels" and opens the level list.
StayCloses the card. The room stays as built, ready to edit.
Tap outside the card, or BackSame as Stay.

On a phone the card rises from the bottom, so the room and the robot on its goal stay in view above it. On a computer it sits at the bottom of the room. The goals card at the top of the level still ticks off each goal as now.
