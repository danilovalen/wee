Achievements: screen and popup

Sketch for registers 629-637. Left: the Achievements page. Right: what a win looks like when it earns one. Copy is a draft. Every achievement shown here gives +1 star except the campaign ones, which keep their counts.

Its own page, opened from the menu (and from the star count on Looks). Done ones are gold and say where they were earned; the rest are dimmed, with a bar where there is something to count. A gold rim marks one earned since your last visit.

The robot reaches the berry, the win card comes up, then the popup drops in at the top for 3 seconds. Tap it to open Achievements. Two earned at once queue, one after the other.

  1. A Run that wins is checked against every achievement not yet done, the same moment the stars are saved.
  2. Each new one: a popup at the top, under the header, so it covers neither the room nor the win card's buttons. It slides in, stays 3 s, slides out. Tap opens Achievements; a swipe up or any tap elsewhere puts it away.
  3. Several at once: one after another, never stacked. The star count on the win card goes up as each lands.
  4. It also lights the red dot on the menu's Achievements button until that page is opened (the same rule as register 628's dot on Looks).
  5. Never during the tutorial, the Sandbox's Test, or a replay.
