A. A map (from Baba Is You)

  • The builder levels are the main road, opened one by one by beating the one before.
  • A beaten builder level grows a short branch to the puzzle or challenge it opened; the branch is not there before.
  • Under each beaten node, its side-goal stars appear (filled when met). An unbeaten node shows none.
  • Star rewards live in one place on the map, the Workshop. A pack is an island with its star number; the road never needs stars.
  • The robot can slide along the map's paths to move between nodes, as in Baba Is You.
Good: branching is visible as places; packs feel like places to reach. Costs: map art and layout per world; the most to build.

B. A track (from Duolingo)

  • One column of builder levels: always exactly one "play next", nothing to choose.
  • When a builder level is beaten, a node slides out beside it: the puzzle or challenge it opened. Side levels never block the column.
  • Left of a beaten node: its stars, the win then its side goals, which appear only after the win.
  • The reward line sits at the top; each dot opens at a star total and names what it opens. Packs open there too, and add a new column.
Good: simplest to read on a phone, cheapest to build, no art. Costs: no exploring; packs as extra columns need a way to switch.

C. Today's level list, grown (closest to what exists)

  • Today's list, with each builder card's puzzle or challenge as a card beside it in a second column.
  • The slot beside the next builder level is an empty dashed card saying what opens it ("beat 3"), so the branching is promised before it happens.
  • A builder card shows the win star, a line, then one star per side goal; before the win it shows only the win star (today it shows all of them).
  • Rewards are a row of cards under the list, each with its star number; reached ones are ticked.
Good: the least to build (the list, cards, star rows and level kinds exist). Costs: the least sense of a journey.

Shared by all three

MomentWhat the player sees
Beat a builder levelThe win card names what opened: "Side goals: 2 · Next: level 4 · New: Challenge 2". Back on the campaign, the new nodes or cards arrive with a small pop.
Use a piece for the first timeA one-line note when the level ends: "Box added to the editor". The Sandbox's tool row shows only pieces met so far; unmet ones are not shown at all (not greyed).
Earn an achievementA star with the achievement's name, once. Draft examples: First push (push a box), Shortcut (beat a level in its fewest tiles), Long way round (a 15-move solution), Doorman (go through a door three times in one run).
Reach a reward's star totalThe reward card turns gold; a skin or palette can be picked from Settings, a tool appears in the editor, a pack appears in the campaign.

Unlock everything, this session only (register 589)

  • Where: a small open-padlock icon in the main menu's top-right corner.
  • Opens: every builder level, every side goal, every puzzle and challenge, every pack and reward, every piece in the editor, and the hidden levels. Tap again to lock back.
  • Session only: kept in memory, never written to saved progress; a reload ends it. Stars earned while it is on are real wins and still count.
  • While on: a padlock badge beside the star total on every screen.
  • Gate: a test turns it on, checks a locked level opens, reloads, and checks saved progress is unchanged and the lock is back.
