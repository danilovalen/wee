Three looks for the lab

The same four things in each: a room after a spring slam, the level's clipboard, a page of the blob's album, and two buttons. Pick one, or name the parts to mix. The words on the clipboard and in the album are drafts to show the two voices; none is approved copy.

Every border is doubled in thickness from the first sketch, after Yoshi's Island (register 845). The interface edges wobble between three drawings. The room holds still on purpose: a grid someone has to read should not move. With reduced motion on, nothing wobbles.

The pixel mask (register 846) sits over each whole look and snaps everything to one grid: pieces, panels, lettering, tape and the wobble. Nothing can land between pixels. Switch it off to compare.
