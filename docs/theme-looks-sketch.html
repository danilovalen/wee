Three looks for the lab

The same four things in each: a room where a spring has crushed the blob against the wall behind it, the level's clipboard, a page of the blob's album, and two buttons. Pick one, or name the parts to mix. The words on the clipboard and in the album are drafts to show the two voices; none is approved copy.

Every border is doubled in thickness from the first sketch, after Yoshi's Island (register 845). The interface edges wobble between three drawings. The room holds still on purpose: a grid someone has to read should not move. With reduced motion on, nothing wobbles.

Pixel perfect means every edge and letter sits on one grid. The first try used a mask over the finished picture, which chewed lettering and stretched photos into stray pixels, so it is gone (register 847): everything is now drawn on the grid to begin with, and no outline drops a pixel. Switch it off to see the hand-drawn version with scrawl and wobble.
