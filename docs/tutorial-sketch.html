Tutorial level: sketch (registers 507, 510)

A first level, before Level 1, that walks a new player through one room. The dark bubble is the coach; the ring shows where to tap. Words are drafts.

The plan

  1. Where it lives: "How to play", the first card of the level list. A first-time player opening Campaign lands in it. It can be played again any time, and skipped.
  2. One room, 5×5, only blocks, the start and the star already placed. The bubble waits for each action; a wrong tap does nothing.
  3. Six steps: what stops the robot; the star is the target; you build the room; place one block; Run; the stars on the card.
  4. What it teaches: only blocks and walls stop the robot; you build the room, Run moves the robot; Run checks every shortest path; the big star and the small ones; fewest tiles as the score.
  5. Its own rules: it is won in 2 moves, so it lifts the 4-move minimum every other level has, and has no side goals to meet (the two small stars show what Level 1 adds).
  6. What it leaves for Level 1: the 4-move minimum, Test, the side goals list, resizing the room. Level 1's first open could show one bubble for the minimum.
  7. Built from the game's own pieces: a coach bubble, a ring on the thing to tap, and a step list per level. The same parts could guide the first time a new piece appears (doors, boxes) later.
