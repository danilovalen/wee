Row 406

Tap a slot to open its group

"It's very hard to tap on the bottom right arrow on corner on mobile... Just show the menu on tap direct to the square"

Today a tap on another group's slot picks that group's last tool, and the group's list opens only on a second tap, a hold or a right click. The corner mark looks like the button and is too small to hit.

  • A tap on any slot opens its list; a second tap on it closes it. A tap outside closes it too.
  • A group with one tool (Pieces: the box) picks it at once, with no list.
  • The corner mark stays only as a hint that a list exists.
  • Cost: switching back to a tool you used before takes two taps (open, pick) instead of one.
Decide: is the extra tap acceptable? The other way: the first tap picks the group's last tool AND opens the list, so one tap is enough when the last tool is the one you want.

Row 408 (sketch)

Zoom and the camera

"We need a way to pinch for zooming in and out for bigger levels. We should sketch not only zooming but the experience in general... when to allow zooming in, if the camera follows the player / robot, gesture, and stuff like that."

Why it matters: the Hex counter is 60 by 46 tiles; fitted to a phone that is about 6 px a tile, too small to place anything.

Proposal

QuestionProposal
When is zoom offered?Only when the room, fitted to the screen, gives tiles under about 28 px (a hand-sized target). Small rooms, every campaign level today included, never zoom, so no pinch can go wrong there.
Where does it start?Always fitted: the whole room in view. Zoom in goes up to tiles of about 56 px; zoom out stops at fitted.
Gestures, phonePinch zooms around the point between the fingers; two fingers drag pans. One finger keeps doing what it does now (place, erase, move, swipe to walk), so editing never fights the camera.
Gestures, computerCtrl and the wheel (or a trackpad pinch) zooms at the pointer; the wheel scrolls; space and drag pans.
Getting backA Fit button appears in the room's corner only while zoomed. A two-finger double tap also fits.
Where am I?A small map of the whole room in a corner while zoomed, the view marked on it; tap it to jump there.
Does the camera follow?While playing and zoomed, yes: it follows whoever moves, the robot in a run, you in Puzzles and a played Sandbox room. It holds still while the mover stays in the middle of the screen (the dashed box) and slides only when they would leave it, so the room does not swim on every step. A pan with two fingers pauses following until the next move.
Editing and playingPlay keeps the zoom; leaving play returns to the view you were editing with.
The room's edgesThe add and remove bars stay on the room's edges and move with it; when an edge is off screen, so are its bars.
Decide: three choices, in order: (1) camera follows with the dead zone, or always keeps the mover centred, or never follows? I recommend the dead zone. (2) Is the mini map wanted, or just Fit? (3) Should zoom also be allowed on small rooms, for a closer look?
