Level 3's goals. The level list already says it the right way: one big star, a line, then one small star per side goal. Each option below repeats that shape on the goal card, so the card and the list read the same. Words are drafts.

A. Two boxes (recommended)

The big star sits in a gold box with everything it needs, none of them marked one by one until a Run. Each side goal is its own chip that begins with its own small star, and that star fills when the goal is met. One big star, five small stars: the same count as on the level card.

"At least 4 moves"

Four dots said "four of something", not "at least four moves". Three ways to say it:

1. A path mark and 4+ (recommended)
4+

A bent path with an arrow is "moves". During a Run it counts up: 1, 2, 3 in grey, then 4+ turns green.

2. The robot and 4+
4+

Shortest, but "robot 4+" could read as four robots.

3. Words: 4+ moves
4+ moves

Plain and unmistakable; one word.
